Twilio | Square01

Multi-Tenant Restaurant Waitlist

This case study covers a multi-tenant SaaS that replaces paper waitlists for walk-in restaurants. Guests join from a QR code or from a widget on the restaurant's website, and they get a phone call from Twilio when their table is ready. Wait times come from live Square POS data: today's open dine-in checks are compared with the restaurant's table count, and webhooks with signature checks refresh the estimate the moment a check opens or closes. Each restaurant's Square credentials are encrypted separately, every table uses row-level security, and the trial model is enforced in the database.

Google Polygons03

Multi-Branch Pizza Platform with Scheduled Promotions, Delivery Zones & Pizza Builder

This case study covers a bilingual website and back office for a 30-branch pizza chain in Nuevo León, Mexico. Banners and promotions publish and expire on their own, based on dates, weekdays and corporate or per-branch targeting, and their images are served through short-lived signed links. Each branch draws its delivery areas on Google Maps as polygons or radius zones, with a fee and minimum order per zone that the server matches to the customer's address. Customers build their own pizza with a live SVG preview, and the owners manage menus, options, prices, images and branch features from a role-based admin panel.
