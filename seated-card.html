Seated — Multi-Tenant Restaurant Waitlist with Square-Powered Wait Estimates

This case study covers a multi-tenant SaaS that replaces paper waitlists for walk-in restaurants. Guests join from a QR code or from a widget on the restaurant's website, and they get a phone call from Twilio when their table is ready. Wait times come from live Square POS data: today's open dine-in checks are compared with the restaurant's table count, and webhooks with signature checks refresh the estimate the moment a check opens or closes. Each restaurant's Square credentials are encrypted separately, every table uses row-level security, and the trial model is enforced in the database.
